Methodology
• Multi-Source Data Collection: Collect radar, satellite, lightning, atmospheric observation and numerical weather model data.
• Data Pre-processing: Perform quality control, noise removal, missing-value handling, normalization and temporal synchronization.
• Spatio-Temporal Data Fusion: Align and combine observations from multiple sources into a unified spatio-temporal representation.
• Feature Extraction: Extract radar, cloud, lightning and atmospheric features related to thunderstorm formation, movement and intensity.
• AI-Based Nowcasting: Use AI/ML models to predict thunderstorm development, movement, intensity and lightning probability over short time horizons.
• Risk Mapping & Early Warning: Convert predictions into localized risk zones, confidence levels and timely alerts for disaster ops.
